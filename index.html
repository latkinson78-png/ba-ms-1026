Staff email signatures

Mobile-friendly signatures for Outlook on iPhone and iPad. Choose your name, copy the rendered signature, then paste it into your Bounce Abilities account signature.

  1. Tap your name below.
  2. Tap Copy signature.
  3. Open Outlook → profile icon → Settings → Signature.
  4. If you use multiple accounts, enable the per-account signature option and choose your Bounce Abilities account.
  5. Paste the signature and send yourself a test email.
